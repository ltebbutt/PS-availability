About

A cookie is a small file of letters and numbers that we store on your browser or the hard drive of your device if you agree. Cookies contain information that is transferred to your computer's hard drive.

We use the following cookies:

  • Necessary cookies. These cookies are required for the operation and functionality of our website allowing functions like page navigation and access to the secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Preference cookies. These allow a website to remember information that changes the way the website behaves or looks, like your preferred language or the region that you are
  • Statistic cookies. They allow website owners to understand how visitors interact with the websites by collecting and reporting information anonymously
  • Marketing cookies. These are used to track visitors across the website. The intention is to display adverts that are relevant and engaging for the individual user.

 

Setting your preferences

You will then be provided a pop-up page with 2 options. Select the green button to allow all cookies, or the white button to allow your preferences you have chosen from the list.
