Compare & Buy Consoles

The Eighth Generation - Seven Years On

When they started to trickle into our living rooms way back in 2012, 8th generation video game consoles emphasised home entertainment, immersive gaming, "social media" and high quality graphics. Now that we are at the second, high-powered iteration of Sony and Microsoft's offerings, as well as the innovative and exciting Nintendo Switch, where should you be investing your hard-earned cash? Which system is best for you?

If you are looking for a high-quality gaming experience, then you have a decision to make. A kind of Beano or Dandy, FIFA or PES kind of choice. Do you go for Sony's Playstation 4 or for rival Microsoft's Xbox One? Their decades-long competition has meant that by this stage yes, there are quite similar performance statistics up against each other, which is why my advice to you would be to check out which titles you can play with which before buying. Perusing the Xbox One offering up against the PS4's will help you choose wisely so that you don't block yourself from the game you love.

On top of that it's also key to bear in mind that cross-platform multiplayer is not possible on the majority of these titles, so coordinate your buying decision with gaming buddies before going to checkout.

Tasty Bundles

Bargain hunters may want to check out the different bundles available, as this is where the real savings can be found. If you compare and poke around you can find some great deals on some of the 5-star classics that have been released in the past while, such as Red Dead Redemption 2, or the latest FIFA, Call of Duty or Forza, depending on whether you like to spend your free time shooting balls, shooting bullets, or just shooting around the racetrack.

PlayStation 4 - Which Do I Go For?

The most popular console now gives you more than just the choice of regular or slim and what size capacity - you can now opt for the high-powered PS4 Pro if you really want the top-of-the-line gaming experience.

  • So when should you opt for one? Well, now that it has been out for a few years, the price has tumbled presently, and you can now pick up one for around £300, not too much more than the regular version.
  • But why the Pro? Well, simply put, 4K gaming. If you have or are considering a 4K TV (which are also tumbling price if you shop smart online), then this is a marriage made in heaven. On top of that, boasting a terabyte of internal memory is basically a prerequisite, given the size of games these days, especially if you want to download instead of buying physical disks. As well as overall longevity from having better tech in your console, the PS4 Pro will also be key to the next level of gaming - Virtual Reality. The PlayStation VR is the most affordable high-end headset, and the range of famous compatible games will only expand, with the likes of Gran Turismo: Sport already available.

PS4 controllers are optimised for FPS gaming, with the DualShock 4's larger and more robust with dimpled thumbsticks for better grip. Between the D pad and buttons, Sony has added a six-axis, clickable touchpad – new potential for interacting with games. Yet another enhancement is the light bar, which is used in conjunction with the PS4 camera to track player position (so that you can swap seats with a co-player mid-game). Happily though, this device is too mainstream to go for a lot of gimmicks which can either help or hinder - it focusses on giving you a smooth, powerful experience.

What's up in Xbox?

Keen to match their rivals, Microsoft originally marketed the Xbox One as an all-in-one home entertainment system, with expanded Xbox Live Services, enhanced Kinect and access to the cloud for streaming, sharing and recording gaming highlights. More importantly though, it had to match the PS4 Pro and fully engage with 4K gaming and the associated demands of it - so in 2017 we saw the release of first the Xbox One S, a pretty, sleek device which although supporting Ultra HD gaming doesn't really measure up to the PlayStation's graphics power. As a result, "Project Scorpio" (no link to the Simpsons character, unfortunately) was announced, and emerged not long after as the Xbox One X.

The X matches its rival's capability and in several cases has more impressive stats, such as in terms of graphical CPU performance, where it outstrips Sony's offering. However, I will say that both are so high spec that it is difficult to notice the difference, for the most part. Price-wise it is almost identical to the Pro's, so it won't help you make up your mind. In terms of 4K itself, not every game out there is natively developed at that resolution, moreover that they are upscaled. However, every brand new game is, so it will get more and more rewarding in terms of truly jaw-dropping graphics on your tv.

In terms of playing, the controller supports rumble effects for direct feedback. It also sports a 4-way direction pad and buttons more suitable for non-gaming activities. The Kinect sensor now has a 1080p, wide-angle camera and can track face and body gestures, displacement of bodyweight and heart beat. Because it is capable of tracking up to six skeletons, the system supports 6-player, simultaneous gaming. Furthermore, a built-in array mic helps players hone in on the player who is speaking.

Advantages & Disadvantages

Xbox One:

  • This was originally the most expensive 8th generation platform, primarily due to the built-in Kinect sensor, but prices have predictably tumbled. Microsoft hoped that this feature would motivate developers to experiment and produce more titles that made use of its potential for extended functionality. However, the company is now also offering a version of Xbox One without Kinect for a lower price. Though Microsoft originally planned on implementing strict online requirements and used game restrictions, this policy has been softened. One of the top benefits of this platform is its extensive online services.

PlayStation 4:

  • PlayStation is often hailed as the platform of true gamers, especially fans of FPS and action series. Reputed as offering the best, most immersive graphics experience, it convinces through sheer power. Sony has also been lauded for announcing that its PS4 platform will have no online requirements or used game restrictions. You can also download games and content through the PlayStation Store if you don't want to trek to the shops or order online - just keep an eye on your internal memory, and check the speed of your internet connection first!

What's new, Wii U?

Although overtaken by its flashier, more innovative brother, Nintendo's Wii U still offers a lot on its predecessor, including HD graphics support, WiFi and an updated control system. Two versions are currently available. The Basic Pack includes a white console with 8 GB internal storage, HDMI cable, sensor bar for Wii remotes, AC adapters and of course the GamePad. The Premium Pack is arguably the better buy. It comprises a black console with 32 GB storage, all the accessories included in the Basic Set, plus stands, a charging cradle and "Nintendo Land". Home entertainment options on the Wii U include Nintendo TVii, Wii U Chat, Internet browsing, access to third-party services (e.g., Netflix) and a new Wii gaming community called Miiverse, where players can discuss gaming strategy, discover new content and share experiences.

Which is the Best Handheld?

  • Can't really live without that extra bit of gaming on the bus and train but your smartphone isn't doing it for you? Then a handheld is the device of choice - these handy devices ensure gaming fun anywhere. Once insanely popular back in the glorious days of the Game Boy, these days the selection of handhelds is manageable: Microsoft has completely stayed out of the market, and these days there are only a few new games left for the ageing Sony PS Vita. Thankfully, Nintendo has several handhelds in its range: obviously the new Switch console is suitable both for gaming on the go and at home, but there are others.

  • These include the likes of the 3DS, 3DS XL and the 2DSXL. The 3DS XL is a much more capable device than its predecessor, due to a larger display and improved ergonomics, but battery life and a relatively low resolution keep it from achieving a perfect score. The Nintendo 2DS is a less expensive option that caters to children in many ways. It plays 3DS titles and has abandoned the hinged clamshell style in favour of a larger, more ergonomic design. When it comes to buying these, keep an eye out for some decent Nintendo bundle deals to save yourself a few quid and avoid the multiple delivery costs.

Glossary

Type

Game Console refers to all freestanding machines available such as the Sony PS5, and the Xbox Series X/S. Meanwhile, handheld identifies those devices you can carry around with you, like the PlayStation Vita plus all ranges of the Nintendo 2DS and 3DS. The Nintendo Switch is more of a hybrid, being both handheld and able to be connected to a TV. Specific bundles are consoles packaged together in an exclusive box with a specific game title or two, depending on the offers.

Gaming Control

Physically interacting with games in a motion-controlled way is somewhat popular as both Microsoft and Sony offer these capabilities on their machines, and Nintendo based a lot of the Switch around that. Voice control is a handy feature if the couch temporarily ate your controller, in which case the machine's microphone listens to your commands. Also, touchpad operation is possible on many handheld devices as well as on the PS5 and PS4 controllers.

Solid State Storage Capacity

High-end machines such as the PS5 and Xbox Series X/S require installation of games on their internal solid-state drives and considering the average title is around 60 GB in size, a storage space of up to 1 TB will satisfy most gamers. Moreover, both consoles' available storage spaces can be expanded to store more games simultaneously. For the PS5, one could simply install a 3rd party internal SSD that supports the speeds and connection types the platform supports. For the Xbox side, you need to purchase a propriety storage expansion card and connect it via the storage expansion socket.

Other Attributes

Exciting possibilities await in cross-platform gaming, especially if the major market players truly join forces, but it is still quite limited apart from the major titles. In an ideal world this means that if you have a PS5 you can easily play your friend who has an Xbox Series X or Series S and another on his PC.
