PS5 Pro Console FAQs

Checking our stock checker is the quickest way to see current PS5 Pro Console availability across UK retailers.

Most major UK retailers receive regular PS5 Pro Console deliveries, although exact restock dates are rarely announced in advance. If the console is currently unavailable, our stock checker can notify you as soon as it's back in stock.

What is the price of the PS5 Pro Console?

The PS5 Pro Console has a recommended retail price of £699.99 in the UK. While discounts are uncommon, retailers may occasionally offer bundle deals that include games, accessories, or PlayStation Store credit.

What comes with the PS5 Pro Console?

The PS5 Pro Console includes the console itself, one DualSense Wireless Controller, a USB charging cable, an HDMI cable, an AC power cable, horizontal stand feet, and pre-installed Astro's Playroom. A vertical stand and compatible disc drive are sold separately.

Does the PS5 Pro Console include a disc drive?

No. The PS5 Pro Console is supplied as a digital-only console and does not include an Ultra HD Blu-ray Disc Drive. If you want to play physical PS5 or PS4 game discs, you'll need to purchase the official PS5 Disc Drive separately.

What are the improvements in the PS5 Pro Console?

The PS5 Pro features a more powerful GPU, advanced ray tracing, AI-powered PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), and improved overall graphics performance. Many supported games offer higher resolutions, smoother frame rates, and enhanced visual quality compared to the standard PS5.

Is the PS5 Pro worth buying?

The PS5 Pro is designed for players who want the best possible PlayStation gaming experience, particularly on 4K displays. If you value improved graphics, smoother gameplay, and enhanced performance in supported titles, it offers a noticeable upgrade over the standard PS5.

Which games are enhanced for the PS5 Pro?

Many first-party and third-party games have received PS5 Pro enhancements, with developers continuing to release updates for additional titles. Games labelled as PS5 Pro Enhanced have been optimised to take advantage of the console's improved hardware.

Which UK retailers usually stock the PS5 Pro Console?

Major retailers including PlayStation Direct, Amazon UK, Argos, Currys, Very, John Lewis, EE, and ShopTo regularly stock the PS5 Pro Console. Availability varies between retailers, so our stock checker monitors stock across major UK retailers in one place.

Why is the PS5 Pro Console difficult to find?

Demand often spikes during major game launches, holiday shopping periods, and promotional events, causing stock to sell out quickly at some retailers. Limited retailer allocations can also result in temporary shortages, particularly for bundles.
