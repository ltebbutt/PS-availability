Product description

Product code: 82640431

With the PlayStation5 Pro console, the world’s greatest game creators can enhance their games with incredible features like advanced ray tracing, super sharp image clarity for your 4K TV, and high frame rate gameplay.* That means you get to play PS5 games with the most impressive visuals ever possible on a PlayStation console, and with 2TB of SSD storage included**, your favourite games will be ready and waiting for you to jump right in for your next awesome adventure.

All-digital console
PS5 Pro is an all-digital console with no disc drive. Sign into your account for PlayStation Network and go to PlayStation Store to buy and download games.*** You can also add a Disc Drive to your PS5 Pro console if you would like to play PS5 or PS4 games on Blu-ray Disc, or if you want to watch movies and shows on 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray Discs, Blu-ray Discs, and DVDs. The compatible Disc Drive is sold separately.

2TB of storage
Keep your favourite games ready and waiting for you to jump in and play with 2TB of SSD storage built in.**

Play at 60fps, or up to 120fps, with 4K Output and Ray Tracing for compatible games 
With the power of the PS5 Pro, compatible games can be played at 60fps, or up to 120fps, with Ray Tracing and AI enhanced 4K resolution using PSSR—all at the same time—on your 4K TV.

PS5 Pro Game Boost and Backwards Compatibility
The PS5 Pro console can play over 8,500 PS4 games. With the PS5 Pro version of the Game Boost feature, you can even enjoy faster and smoother frame rates in some of the PS4 and PS5 console’s greatest games.****

8K output
If you own an 8K television, you'll be pleased to learn that PS5 consoles support 8K output, so your eyes can feast on games playing on your 4320p resolution display.

HDR colours
PS5 games can display an unbelievably vibrant and lifelike range of colours, thanks to the console supporting HDR technology.

3D audio
Listening either through your headphones or TV speakers, Tempest 3D AudioTech will enable your surroundings to truly come alive in supported PS5 games. Soundscapes emit from every direction to help you immersive into the game's environment.

* Features only available on select PS5 games that have been enhanced for PS5 Pro when compared with PS5. PS5 Pro enhanced features will vary by game.
** A portion of the SSD is reserved for system software and other functions so the available SSD capacity may vary.
*** Account for PlayStation Network required; account holders must be 7 years or older and under 18s require parental consent. Full terms apply.
**** There are a small number of PS4 games that are not compatible with PS5 Pro.


DualSense Wireless Controller
Retaining many familiar DUALSHOCK 4 features, the DualSense wireless controller for PlayStation 5 has been designed for a whole new generation. Dual actuators replace traditional rumble motors, adaptive triggers provide varying levels of force and tension, and sound effects can burst directly from the controller's integrated speaker – all to enable you with an incredibly immersive gaming experience.

Next gen feedback
The DualSense wireless controller replaces traditional rumble motors for dual actuators, which allow you to feel physical, responsive feedback to your in-game actions, simulating the feeling of everything from environments to the recoil of different weapons.

Adaptive triggers
Feel physically connected to your on-screen actions, thanks to the DualSense's Adaptive triggers. From pulling back an increasingly tight bowstring to hitting the brakes on a speeding car, you'll feel varying levels of force and tension.

Chat away
Using either the controller's built-in microphone or by connecting a headset to the 3.5mm jack, you can easily chat with friends. You can even switch voice capture on and off with the dedicated mute button.

Create more
Building on the success of the SHARE button, use the DualSense' create button to broadcast your most epic gaming moments.

Designed for comfort
The DualSense has been created with a two-tone design that compliments your console. With an intuitive layout featuring enhanced sticks, a reimagined light bar, along with a built-in accelerometer and gyroscope, you're all set for those end of level bosses.
